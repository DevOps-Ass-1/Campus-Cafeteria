How to Place Your Order

Follow our quick step-by-step process for a seamless cafeteria experience.

Ordering Steps

  1. Browse the Menu: Check the electronic display board or our website for available dishes and daily deals.
  2. Pay at Counter: Proceed to Cash Counter 1 or 2. Choose cash or digital debit card payment and receive your printed order token.
  3. Token Calling: Keep an eye on the digital order display screen above the pickup area.
  4. Collect Your Meal: When your token number lights up, present your token at the pickup counter and collect your food.

Cafeteria Rules & Guidelines

  • Peak Rush Hours: Expect higher wait times during 11:30 AM – 1:30 PM.
  • Self-Service Policy: Please return your food trays and trash to the designated disposal racks after eating.
  • Order Pickup: Orders must be collected within 10 minutes of display to ensure food stays hot.
